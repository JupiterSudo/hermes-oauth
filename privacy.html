Hermes privacy policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Hermes is a personal automation assistant. Its developer runs it for their own use only, and the only Google account it connects to is the developer's own (in this policy, "the owner"). This policy explains how Hermes accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Google data Hermes accesses

Access requestedWhat Hermes does with it

gmail.readonly		Reads the owner's messages to find booking, meeting and other emails the owner wants handled.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide Hermes's own features to its single owner, as described above. Hermes acts only on the owner's instructions or on schedules the owner has set up.

Google user data is never:

  • sold or rented, or used for advertising, including retargeting or personalised or interest-based advertising;
  • used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • used to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models, or transferred to anyone for that purpose.

No one other than the owner reads Google user data.

Sharing

Hermes does not transfer, share or disclose Google user data to third parties, except where this is needed to carry out a task the owner has asked for:

  • AI language-model processing. Hermes uses a cloud large-language-model service to understand the owner's requests and the content involved. When a task needs Google data, Hermes sends only the relevant part (for example, the text of one booking email) to that service to produce the result. It is sent for no other purpose.
  • Replies to the owner. Hermes may report results to the owner through the owner's own private chat account.

Hermes may also disclose data if the law requires it. There are no other transfers.

Storage and retention

  • Hermes has no servers of its own. It runs on, and keeps its data on, the owner's own computer, not on any server operated for Hermes.
  • The Google authorisation tokens are stored in a file on that computer that only the owner's user account can read. They are kept until access is revoked or the owner deletes them.
  • Hermes does not copy the owner's mailbox, calendar, contacts or files into a separate database. It fetches what a task needs at the time. Extracts that appear in Hermes's conversation history stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them.
  • Hermes talks to Google APIs over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Revoking access and deleting data

Hermes's access to the Google account can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, Hermes can no longer read or change any Google data. Deleting Hermes's local data directory removes the stored tokens and any locally held extracts.

Compliance with Google's policy

Hermes's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
