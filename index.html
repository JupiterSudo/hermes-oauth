Hermes

Hermes is a personal automation assistant that connects to its owner's own Google account and handles routine mail, calendar and document tasks for them.

Privacy policyTerms of service

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail and files it: applies labels, archives and marks messages as read.
  • Spots booking confirmations and meeting emails and creates or updates the matching events in the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Reads rows from, and appends rows to, the owner's Google Sheets, and reads and appends text in the owner's Google Docs. It uses Google Drive to find those files.
  • Sends an email from the owner's account when the owner tells it to, and looks up addresses in the owner's Google Contacts (read-only) to do so.

Hermes runs on the owner's own computer and only acts on the owner's instructions or on schedules the owner has set up.

Who it is for

Hermes has exactly one user: its owner and developer, who uses it with their own Google account. It is not offered, sold or distributed to the public. Nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

Google data

Hermes asks for access to Gmail (read, send and modify), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Contacts (read-only). The privacy policy sets out how each is used, stored and protected. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
